# SmartBursary User Guide

## For Applicants

### Getting Started

#### 1. Create an Account
1. Visit https://smart-bursary.vercel.app/
2. Click "Create an account"
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone Number
   - ID Number
   - Select your Constituency
   - Select your Ward
   - Create a strong password
4. Click "Create Account"
5. You'll be automatically logged in

#### 2. Complete Your Profile
Before you can apply for bursaries, you must complete your profile:

1. Go to "My Profile"
2. Fill in all required fields:
   - **Personal Information:**
     - Date of Birth
     - National ID Number
     - Gender
     - County, Sub-County, Ward
   
   - **Education Information:**
     - Institution Name
     - Student Number
     - Course
     - Year of Study
     - Admission Year
   
   - **Family Information:**
     - Monthly Household Income
     - Household Size
     - Guardian Name, Phone & Relationship
   
3. Upload required documents:
   - National ID or Birth Certificate
   - Student ID Card
   - Admission Letter
4. Click "Save Changes"

#### 3. Browse Available Bursaries
1. Go to "Browse Bursaries"
2. You'll see bursaries available for your constituency
3. Click on any bursary to see details:
   - Description
   - Eligibility criteria
   - Amount (if disclosed)
   - Deadline
   - Required documents

#### 4. Apply for a Bursary
1. Click "Apply Now" on a bursary
2. Review pre-filled information
3. Write your reason for applying
4. Submit application
5. Note your Application Number (e.g., SB-2026-00001)

#### 5. Track Your Applications
1. Go to "My Applications"
2. See all your applications with status:
   - **Submitted:** Application received
   - **Under Review:** Being reviewed by constituency officer
   - **Verification:** Documents being verified
   - **Committee Review:** Under committee consideration
   - **Approved:** Congratulations! Download award letter
   - **Rejected:** Application not approved
   - **Disbursed:** Funds sent to your institution

#### 6. Download Award Letter
Once approved:
1. Go to "My Applications"
2. Click on the approved application
3. Click "Download Award Letter"
4. Print and submit to your institution

## For Reviewers

### Dashboard
View statistics for your constituency:
- Total applications
- Pending reviews
- Approved applications
- Rejected applications

### Review Applications
1. Go to "Review Applications"
2. See applications from your constituency only
3. Click on an application to review
4. Check:
   - Applicant details
   - Documents uploaded
   - Financial need analysis
   - Priority score

### Make a Decision
1. Add reviewer comments
2. Select action:
   - **Approve:** Application meets criteria
   - **Reject:** Does not meet criteria
   - **Request More Info:** Need additional documents
3. Click "Update Status"

### Priority Scoring
Applications are automatically scored (0-100) based on:
- **Financial Need (70%):** Household income vs household size
- **Education Need (20%):** Course relevance, year of study
- **Risk Assessment (10%):** Duplicate detection, completeness

## Common Questions

### Why can't I see any bursaries?
You can only see bursaries from your home constituency. Make sure your constituency is set correctly in your profile.

### Why can't I apply?
Complete your profile to 95%+ and upload all required documents (National ID, Student ID, Admission Letter).

### How long does review take?
Review times vary by constituency. Typically 2-4 weeks. Check "My Applications" for real-time updates.

### Can I apply for multiple bursaries?
Yes! You can apply for as many bursaries as you're eligible for.

### What if my application is rejected?
You can apply again in the next cycle or apply for different bursaries.
